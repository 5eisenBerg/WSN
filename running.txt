Why DQN HP Delay is 0

In simulator logic, HP delay is forced to 0.0 when no HP packets are delivered: wsn_100dynamic.cc:146-164.
Your DQN policy collapsed to always action 1 (send normal): DQN_actions.txt has {1: 2499} (100%), while action 0 is the only HP-send path in wsn_100dynamic.cc:266-277.
Plotting code also masks empties by writing 0.0 into empty HP delay files before CDF plotting: run_final_pipeline.py:372-379. That is why DQN_hp_delays.txt ended up containing only 0.0.
